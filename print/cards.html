Red Flag: print and play

Spot the reasoning error in a research claim, or call it sound. Version 0.1 (prototype). 115 cards in standard poker size (63 × 88 mm), 9 per A4 page.

Reference3 cards1 pages
Claims60 cards7 pages
Flags30 cards4 pages
Checks16 cards2 pages
Sound card6 cards1 pages

Printing. Print at 100% / actual size with background graphics on. Cut along the card edges. All cards share one back (backs.html): print it on the reverse of each sheet, or skip it and use opaque card sleeves.

Answers are in the separate answer-key booklet (answer-key.html), so the top card of a face-down deck never gives its answer away.

Reference · page 1
One round
  • 1Flip the top Claim card. Everyone reads it.
  • 2Race to slap down the Flag that names its error, or your Sound card. First card down claims the call.
  • 3Advanced: add the Check card that would expose your Flag.
  • 4Read the key aloud: score, argue, appeal. Next player flips.
Four families of flags
  • Cause Errors in reading cause and effect from data.
  • Sample Errors about who or what was studied, and how far the result reaches.
  • Numbers Errors in reading statistics, risks and charts.
  • Practice Errors in how the research was analysed, measured and reported.
Scoring
  • 🥇 Best +3
  • 🥈 Defensible +1
  • ⚠️ Trap -2. Flagging a sound claim, or calling a flawed one sound, is always a trap.
  • ⚖️ Unlisted 0, or +1 if the table upholds your appeal
  • 🔍 Check +1 if your Check card exposes your Flag on this claim
